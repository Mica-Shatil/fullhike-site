How to report a problem in-app

The fastest way to report a bug, weird rule call, or visual glitch is right inside Full Hike: Sail Racing, because the report automatically captures the race seed and wind conditions needed to reproduce it.

During a race

  1. Tap the Pause button in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Report a problem below the pause menu.
  3. Pick a category: Bug, Looks wrong, Confusing, or Feels wrong.
  4. Write a short note on what happened, tap Copy report, and paste it into an email to support@fullhike.com.

From the home screen

  1. Open Settings from the home screen.
  2. Tap Report a problem (your app version is shown right beneath it).
  3. Choose a category and add a short description.
  4. Tap Copy report and paste it into an email to support@fullhike.com.

Store vs. beta builds: App Store and Google Play versions have Copy report so nothing is uploaded automatically — paste it into an email to support@fullhike.com. Beta test builds also include Send report, which uploads directly (or queues locally while offline). See our Privacy Policy for the exact list of fields included.

Controls, Rig & Difficulty FAQ

How do the touch controls work?

Hold your phone in landscape. Everything is designed for two thumbs:

  • Steer (left side): Put your left thumb anywhere on the lower-left area and slide left or right to turn. Lift your thumb to hold your heading straight.
  • Tack or gybe (swipe up): Make a quick upward swipe on the left steering side to tack upwind or gybe downwind cleanly through the wind.
  • Auto Trim vs. Manual Trim: By default, AUTO trims your mainsail for you while you focus on steering, wind shifts, and tactics. Tap the control mode button (bottom-right) or switch in Settings to enable MANUAL trim, which adds a vertical Sheet strip for big adjustments and a quarter-circle Fine trim wheel for small tweaks.
  • Look around (right side): Drag on open water on the right side of the screen to pan the camera and check rivals or wind puffs. It eases back behind your boat after release, or double-tap (or tap RECENTER) to snap back smoothly.

New to sailing? Tap Learn to Sail on the home screen for six short, hands-on lessons covering steering, wind, tacking, mark roundings, gybing, and starting.

What do the rig settings (Vang, Cunningham, Outhaul) do in plain words?

Tap RIG near the bottom-right before the start or during a race to shape your Club Dinghy's mainsail for the breeze. The panel shows a live SPEED % readout so you can see whether your setup is helping or hurting right now.

Quick one-tap presets (for upwind):

  • LIGHT (under 9 knots): Makes the sail deep and full for extra power in light air.
  • MEDIUM (9–15 knots): Balanced all-round sail shape (the default).
  • HEAVY (over 15 knots): Flattens the sail to shed excess heel and drag in strong gusts.
  • DOWNWIND: One-tap button at the top of the panel that eases the sail for reaching and running after you round the top mark.

Individual controls (3 steps each):

  • Vang (Kicker) — Soft / Med / Hard: Pulls the boom down. Upwind it bends the mast slightly to flatten the sail; downwind it keeps the boom from lifting so the top of the sail doesn't twist open and spill power.
  • Cunno (Cunningham) — Off / Med / On: Tightens the front edge (luff) of the sail to pull fullness forward and flatten the sail upwind in stronger wind. Ease it off downwind.
  • Outhaul — Loose / Med / Tight: Controls how deep or flat the bottom edge (foot) of the sail is. Loose gives punchier acceleration and power in light air; tight flattens the sail and reduces heeling in a breeze.
How do Easy, Normal, and Hard rival difficulties work?

Every boat in the fleet — yours and the three computer boats (Gull, Tern, and Skua) — sails the exact same Club Dinghy with identical physics. Bots never get secret speed boosts.

  • Easy: Rivals start a bit later after the gun, trim their sails a little looser, steer slightly wider upwind angles, react more slowly to wind shifts, and leave their rig setup alone after the start.
  • Normal: Realistic club-fleet competition — Gull pushes hard and reads the breeze well, Tern sails solidly in the middle, and Skua makes occasional novice mistakes.
  • Hard: Rivals nail tighter starts, trim closer to optimum speed, play wind shifts sooner, steer steadier courses, and tune their rig accurately for both the beat and the run.
Why isn't sound or vibration working on my phone?
  • Open Settings from the home screen and confirm both Sound and Haptics are switched ON.
  • On iPhone, Full Hike respects your hardware silent switch — flip the ringer switch off silent (or raise media volume) to hear wind, water, and race horns. Your own background music or podcasts can keep playing alongside the game.
  • On Android, confirm media volume is up and system touch/vibration feedback isn't muted by Do Not Disturb or Battery Saver.
I got a penalty or OCS (On Course Side) — what do I do?
  • OCS (crossed the start line early): Sail back completely behind the start line (below the pin and committee boat) and cross again cleanly. While returning, keep clear of boats that started properly.
  • Mark touch (Rule 31): Complete a one-turn penalty (one tack and one gybe, 360°) clear of other boats.
  • Right-of-way foul (e.g., port/starboard Rule 10, windward/leeward Rule 11, mark-room Rule 18): Complete a two-turn penalty (720°) once clear of traffic. The on-screen indicator tracks your turns until cleared.
