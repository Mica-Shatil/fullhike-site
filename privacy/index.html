Privacy Policy

Applies to: fullhike.com & Full Hike: Sail Racing (iOS & Android) · Effective date: 22 November 2026

The short version: On fullhike.com, we use zero cookies, zero analytics, and zero trackers. If you join the beta waitlist, we collect only your email address (processed via Google Forms) solely to let you know when the beta and launch open. In the Full Hike: Sail Racing app, play is offline with no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases at launch, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your settings and race history stay on your phone, and nothing leaves your device unless you open Report a problem and tap Send report.

1. This website (fullhike.com) & waitlist

We keep fullhike.com minimal and private:

  • What we collect: Your email address only, and only if you choose to submit the beta waitlist form on the home page. (The form includes a hidden anti-spam field that is checked in your browser and never sent.)
  • Who processes it: When you tap Join the beta, your browser submits your email address directly to a private Google Form operated by Google LLC, acting as our data processor. Waitlist responses are visible only to the Full Hike owner.
  • Why we collect it: Strictly to email you about the Full Hike closed beta and launch announcement. Your email is never shared, rented, or sold, and we will not send unrelated marketing.
  • No cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts: fullhike.com sets zero cookies, runs zero analytics or advertising scripts, and self-hosts all fonts and images. Browsing the site makes no third-party requests unless you submit the waitlist form to Google Forms.
  • Unsubscribe or delete your email: Reply to any waitlist email to unsubscribe or ask us to delete your email address from the waitlist, and we will remove it promptly.

3. How optional problem reports work

Problem reporting is completely opt-in. You can open it from Settings → Report a problem or from the in-race Pause menu → Report a problem. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap Send report.

A report is a JSON file (capped at 300 KB) containing:

  • A random report ID (UUID) generated solely for that report, plus the UTC time it was created.
  • The category you picked (Bug, Looks wrong, Confusing, or Feels wrong) and any note you typed (up to 2,000 characters). Please do not put personal details in your note.
  • App build details (version, channel, build commit/date), OS platform, device model string, and screen pixel size.
  • Current race state (venue, boat class, course, wind condition, difficulty, phase, race clock, and random race seed) so we can replay the same conditions.
  • A downscaled JPEG screenshot of the game screen (maximum 640 px on the longest side) taken when you open the report sheet. It only captures the game itself — never your camera or photo library.
  • Up to 5 recent local sailing session summaries (control mode, duration, tacks, speed percentage, venue/course).

Current server status: The Cloudflare Worker report receiver is not deployed yet. In builds without a configured report URL, tapping Send report saves the report only in your device's local queue and makes zero network requests. You can use Copy report to share it manually instead.

When the report server (a Cloudflare Worker) is enabled in a future release build:

  • Reports are sent over encrypted HTTPS only, with redirects disabled so requests cannot be downgraded.
  • If your phone is offline, the report waits in the local queue on your device and is retried on a later app launch, then deleted locally after delivery.
  • The server strictly strips any unexpected fields and does not store IP addresses or HTTP headers alongside reports. Cloudflare's edge uses the request IP transiently in memory only to enforce spam rate limits.
  • Reports are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV solely for the developer to triage and fix bugs, and are never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Analytics, ads & third-party tracking

There are none. Full Hike: Sail Racing contains:

  • No advertising SDKs and no ads.
  • No third-party analytics, telemetry, or attribution SDKs.
  • No Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity Cloud Diagnostics, or crash-reporting SDKs enabled.
  • No user accounts, social logins, cloud saves, or cross-app tracking.

5. Device features & permissions

  • Haptics / Vibration: On iPhone, the app uses Apple's native haptic feedback generators (UIImpactFeedbackGenerator and UINotificationFeedbackGenerator). On Android, it uses the system vibrator (android.os.Vibrator / VibratorManager, which uses Android's normal VIBRATE permission when enabled). Haptics are strictly for tactile sailing feedback (tacks, marks, start horn, penalties), can be turned off anytime in Settings, and collect no data.
  • Audio playback (iOS Audio Session): On iOS, the app configures AVAudioSessionCategoryAmbient with mixing enabled so your phone's hardware silent switch mutes the game and your own music or podcasts keep playing. It is playback-only and never accesses the microphone.
  • No sensitive permissions: The app does not ask for or access your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, Bluetooth, or advertising ID.

6. Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Full Hike: Sail Racing is designed to be safe for sailors of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (under COPPA) or under 16 (under GDPR-K):

  • There are no accounts, usernames, chat, leaderboards, or behavioural ads.
  • The app plays completely offline and never sends data automatically.
  • In-app problem reporting is strictly opt-in, does not ask for a name or email address, and asks players not to enter personal information in the free-text note.

If a parent or guardian believes a child accidentally included personal information in a submitted problem report or on the waitlist, please contact us using the details below and we will delete it promptly.

7. Deleting your data

  • Beta waitlist email: Reply to any waitlist email and ask to unsubscribe or be deleted, and we will remove your address from our Google Form list.
  • On your device: Uninstalling Full Hike: Sail Racing (or clearing app storage in Android settings) permanently deletes your settings, race history, session log, and any unsent local reports.
  • Submitted reports: Because reports are not linked to an account or device ID, contact us with the report ID (if you copied it) or the approximate date and note text so we can locate and delete it from our report store.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this page, change the effective date at the top, and call out material changes in the app's release notes on the App Store and Google Play before or when the update ships.
