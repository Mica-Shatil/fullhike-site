Modes & racing rules

Club Dinghy · Two venues
  1. Every day

    Daily Regatta & Race Codes

    A new race every day with local best times, streaks, and offline share cards with Race Codes so friends can sail the same wind and rivals.

  2. 5 races

    Regatta Series

    Five-race Harbour Regatta with low-point scoring and a throw-out after four races.

  3. Home Club & Kingston

    Quick Race & Courses

    Windward-Leeward, Championship, Triangle, and Olympic courses at Home Club and Kingston, Ontario.

  4. 6 lessons

    Learn to Sail

    Six hands-on lessons covering steering, wind, tacking, mark roundings, gybing, and starting on the gun.

  5. Real breeze

    Read the Wind

    Spot dark gusts and glassy lulls sweeping across the water before they hit. Watch your sail telltales stream, steer into lifts, tack on headers, and keep clear air.

  6. Turn penalties

    On-Water Umpire

    30-second starts (OCS), port/starboard, windward/leeward, mark-room, and mark touches with real turn penalties.

One-design Club Dinghy racing at Home Club and Kingston, Ontario.

Simple controls, real depth

Steer with your left thumb and swipe up to tack or gybe cleanly. Start with Trim: Auto while learning, or switch to Manual for a vertical mainsheet strip and fine-trim wheel.

Open Sail Setup anytime for wind presets plus Vang, Cunningham, and Outhaul, and switch between Plain and Sailor terms in Settings.

  • Computer rivals (Gull, Tern, and Skua) sail the exact same Club Dinghy and physics.
  • 100% offline — settings, race history, and streaks stay on your phone.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
