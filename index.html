The course

One race · ~5 min
  1. Start

    Five-minute races

    Quick to start, quick to finish. Thumb-friendly steering and trim, built for a coffee break or the train home.

  2. Beat

    Real shifts & gusts

    The wind is never steady. Spot the dark patch, sail into the puff, and tack on the knock before the fleet does.

  3. Windward mark

    Telltales & trim

    Ease and trim until the telltales stream. The sail tells you when you're fast, just like on the water.

  4. Run

    Race the computer fleet

    Line up against computer boats, fully offline. No signal on the dock? No problem.

  5. Finish

    Fair, no pay-to-win

    Skill wins races. No boosters, no paid upgrades that make your boat faster.

Built by sailors, for sailors
